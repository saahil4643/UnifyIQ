What is innovative

Variant-aware matching

Preserves differences that matter to the traveller instead of merging them away.

Hybrid AI

Rules + text/image embeddings; an LLM only for low-confidence pairs — accurate and cost-controlled.

Content enrichment

LLM merges the best descriptions and images from every supplier.

Multi-objective ranking

Balances conversion, price competitiveness and supplier reliability.

Human-in-the-loop

A review queue turns corrections into training data.
